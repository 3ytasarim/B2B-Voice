What should a law firm's voice agent handle?

A firm can use an AI receptionist to answer public office questions, capture a limited intake, identify the right queue and schedule a consultation under approved rules. Define what happens to the intake before giving the phone workflow access to case management or an attorney's calendar. In particular, decide which incoming requests need identity verification, a check by the conflicts team or a direct conversation with staff.

Start with one practice area and one office. A caller may be a prospective client, a current client, an opposing party or someone with a delivery or scheduling question. Separate the routes early. How can the firm collect enough for a conflict check?

Give the intake team an approved list of names to collect by matter type, such as the prospective client, relevant entities and other parties. Confirm spelling where necessary. Keep the initial question broad enough to identify the practice area without encouraging a detailed statement. The firm can decide whether someone should send documents through a separately approved channel. Keep the intake small even when the caller volunteers details because they believe sharing the full history will speed up a response.

State professional conduct rules can protect information from a prospective client even when no engagement follows. California Rule 1.18 and New York Rule 1.18 illustrate how prospective-client duties, confidentiality and conflicts can depend on the facts and the jurisdiction. Let counsel apply the current rules where the firm practices, including how the phone flow and any disclosures affect intake. A greeting or notice does not by itself resolve these duties. The agent can save an intake request and tell the caller that the firm will review it. It should never tell a caller who else is represented or infer a conflict decision from a matching name. Give the review team a restricted search and decision record. A possible match or an unavailable system should remain pending even if the calendar has an opening. If another person calls about the same event, treat verification and firm review separately before merging anything. A shared surname or telephone number may indicate different parties, and an automated merge can expose one caller's information to someone adverse.

When is a consultation actually scheduled?

  3. Wait for authorized reviewAssign the request and use only the status the firm has approved for the next action.
  4. Verify the calendar actionConfirm the accepted slot, office and reference when the firm's rules permit booking.

A consultation request can be acknowledged when an owned task exists. A tentative time can be held only when the firm permits holds with its own expiration policy. A confirmed consultation needs authorization to schedule and a matching calendar record. State the local date, time and channel or place as approved. The speaker should make clear that the appointment concerns an initial consultation. The firm controls its acceptance and engagement communications.

How should a firm supervise access to calls, summaries and records?

Map where caller data moves from the line to the agent, the intake task, the calendar, vendor logs and any quality review. Decide who needs access and for how long. Give phone workflows limited permissions and protect confidential material in notes, recordings and calendar entries. Ask vendors about retention, subprocessors, incident response, export and deletion before using client data. Confirm whether any recording, transcription or automated disclosure needs special treatment for the actual calls and locations.

How should the agent handle a time-sensitive request?

Use the firm's approved escalation route when a caller says there is a hearing, filing, imminent date or other time-sensitive issue. Capture the caller's stated timing only if the firm's procedure allows and routing can proceed promptly. The workflow should never calculate a legal deadline, advise whether the caller can wait, or treat a callback request as accepted representation. Define who can receive the handoff, what happens outside office hours and what the caller hears if the destination is unavailable.

Accept a request to speak to a human without requiring the caller to repeat the whole intake. Check the transfer result and use the approved alternate path immediately when needed. Measure successful connections, fallback delivery and when staff actually took responsibility. A time-sensitive voicemail needs the firm's explicit process and a corresponding owner. What should a legal intake pilot prove?

In a fictional pilot, a caller asks for a consultation on a commercial lease matter. The receptionist captures the names approved for a conflicts check and saves one intake. A near match comes back from the firm's search, so the request awaits manual review. The caller rings again later and mentions a date they believe is important. The receptionist follows the approved time-sensitive route, and the receiving employee sees the saved intake in the restricted queue. The agent gives no merits assessment or conflict result. Once the firm determines it can offer a consultation, a verified calendar action produces a confirmation.

Repeat the test with an unavailable conflicts system, two unrelated callers with the same name, a repeat request using a shared number, an unavailable attorney and a caller trying to send an unrequested document. Compare each call's result with the assigned intake, the status the caller heard, verified calendar writes and any disclosure. Track completion of eligible intakes, time to human review, duplicate records, confirmation accuracy and wrong-recipient disclosures. Frequently asked questions

Can a law firm use AI to answer new-client calls?

A phone workflow can handle approved administrative tasks and route a limited intake to staff. The firm should approve script, data handling, professional oversight and the escalation process for the intended use.

Can an AI legal receptionist clear conflicts?

The workflow can gather fields for the firm's review. Authorized firm personnel should apply the governing rules and approve further action, especially when a match or incomplete record needs judgment.

Does taking an intake create an attorney-client relationship?

The firm controls engagement decisions and communications. A prospective-client conversation can trigger professional duties depending on applicable rules and circumstances, even when the firm does not take the matter.

Can a voice agent confirm a legal consultation?

Yes, after the firm's required review and authorization and once the calendar records the approved consultation. The firm separately manages any engagement process.

How should the intake handle a caller with a possible deadline?

Use the firm's time-sensitive route and preserve the caller's stated timing if permitted. A lawyer or designated staff member takes the next step. The workflow should not calculate or interpret deadlines.

Should an AI receptionist record every legal intake call?

The firm should decide which records are necessary and apply the applicable recording, confidentiality, access and retention rules. A limited administrative summary may serve the routing purpose.
